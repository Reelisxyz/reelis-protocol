# Reelis Whitepaper

## Vision

Attention is the scarcest thing a maker produces, and today most of its value leaves with the platform that hosts it. Reelis imagines a world where the people who watch, share and rally around a maker can hold a piece of that story, and where the maker keeps a fair slice of the momentum it creates.

## Mission

Give every maker a token of their own on Solana, route a portion of the activity around it back to them, and let them take it out in money they already use, without asking them to manage keys or learn a new toolset.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation

Identity, domain and a public pulse board that reads the Solana market.

### Phase 2: Mint

The token is minted and its address posted on the site for anyone to verify against the chain.

### Phase 3: Rails

Settlement routes are switched on one at a time, starting with the ones that need the least setup from a maker.

### Phase 4: Network

Maker tooling and outside integrations.
